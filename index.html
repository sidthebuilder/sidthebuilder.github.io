Selected Projects
CHRONOS — Ephemeral AI Agent with Verifiable Self-Termination
Rust arkworks TFHE-rs Groth16
  • Composed homomorphic encryption, a Wesolowski verifiable delay function, and a Groth16 erasure proof so an agent's key is released only by sequential work and its destruction is checkable by a third party. Hand-built Poseidon-128 over BN254 and a Poseidon-based AEAD for in-circuit authenticated decryption.
  • Measured: 8,267 R1CS constraints (all load-bearing), 128-byte proofs verifying in 1 ms, VDF throughput constant in T at ~505k squarings/sec; containment invariants checked exhaustively over 1,728 abstract states at startup.
  • Documented limitations: single-party trusted setup, and FHE inference at small scale only (2.29 s per homomorphic multiplication).
  • github.com/ChronosResearch/project-chronos
PAAC — Provably Aligned AI Core
